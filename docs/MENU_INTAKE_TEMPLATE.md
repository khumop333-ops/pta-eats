# Menu intake — send this to each restaurant owner on WhatsApp

Copy everything between the lines, paste the restaurant's name in, and send. When an owner replies, forward/paste their answers to the agent and it will be loaded into Roma for you.

---

Hi! 👋 We're listing your restaurant on **Roma** (roma.africa) so customers in Pretoria Central can order food from you through us. Orders will come to Roma and we'll coordinate delivery with you.

Please reply with the following — a photo of your current menu is also welcome, but please *also* type the prices so we don't make mistakes:

**1. Restaurant details**
- Restaurant name:
- Short description (1 sentence, e.g. "Home-style magwinya and kota in Pretoria Central"):
- Street address:
- Suburb:
- Your WhatsApp number for order coordination:

**2. Your menu** (for each item)
- Item name:
- Price (R):
- Short description (optional):
- Category (e.g. Breakfast / Mains / Kota / Burgers / Sides / Drinks / Desserts):

Example:
- Item name: Magwinya (4 pieces)
- Price: R25
- Description: Freshly fried, served warm
- Category: Breakfast

**3. Photos**
- One good photo of your shop/food for your page (the tastiest one you have!)
- Your logo, if you have one

**4. Trading hours**
- What time do you open and close? (Roma takes orders 08:00–18:00 — tell us if your hours are different)

Thank you! We'll send you a link to your page to approve before it goes live. 🍽️

---

## When replies come back, check each menu has:

- [ ] ≥ 5 items with typed prices (photos alone are not enough — prices get mistyped)
- [ ] A category per item (items are grouped by category on the page)
- [ ] Real street address + suburb (no placeholders)
- [ ] A cover photo (phone photo is fine if it's well-lit)
- [ ] Owner has seen and approved their page link before `is_listed` is switched on
